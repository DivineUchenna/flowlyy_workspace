# Flowlyy — Business Second Brain

This vault is Divine's operating system for **Flowlyy**, an AI automation
agency selling a certification-compliance-tracker (plus six supporting
automations) to construction companies in the UK, expanding to the US.

Read these three files before doing anything else in this vault — they are
the actual operating charter, not background reading:

- [[flowlyy/uche-2.0/system-prompt|system-prompt]] — tone, priorities, rules
- [[flowlyy/uche-2.0/north-star|north-star]] — the two things everything serves
- [[flowlyy/uche-2.0/routing-map|routing-map]] — priority order + where each
  type of item lands

Also check [[flowlyy/uche-2.0/mistakes-to-avoid|mistakes-to-avoid]] before
repeating a pattern that's already been flagged, and add to it when
something goes wrong.

## North star (one line)

1. Zero operatives turned away at the gate (the product promise).
2. First paying client, live.

If a task doesn't serve either, it's admin or noise — still worth doing,
just not worth interrupting higher-priority work for.

## Priority order

Protected creative/learning time (9am–12pm) > outreach (30 min cap) > client
replies (same-day, always) > Upwork (20 min cap) > content > admin. Full
detail in [[flowlyy/uche-2.0/routing-map|routing-map]].

## Hard rules

- **Single source of truth:** live deal status lives in the Notion outreach
  tracker, not in files. `prospects/` and `clients/` are research and
  history — don't let the two drift.
- **Clients only exist once they've paid.** Before that, they're a prospect.
- **Credentials are private.** Never surface, log, or suggest committing
  anything in `credentials/` — it's gitignored, keep it that way.
- **Content is real or it doesn't ship.** No fabricated results, stats, or
  quotes.
- **Write decisions down when they're made**, in `decisions/`, with the
  reasoning — not reconstructed after the fact.

## Where things go

See the full table in [[flowlyy/uche-2.0/routing-map|routing-map]]. Quick
version: unsorted → `_inbox/`, researched-not-contacted → `prospects/<city>/`,
paying → `clients/<name>/`, reusable pitch material → `docs/`, raw stats/
research → `references/`, pricing/niche/positioning calls → `decisions/`,
finished automation builds → `automations/<name>/`, one-off code →
`scripts/`, closed/replaced → `archives/` (moved, never deleted).
