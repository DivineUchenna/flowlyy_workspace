Setup

On your line by the end of the week.

01

Send us your price list

A spreadsheet, a PDF, the back of an invoice. We load your services, rates, service area and working hours into the agent. Under an hour.

02

Ring it yourself and correct it

Call the agent as if you were a customer. Anything it gets wrong you tell it in plain English — "we don't go past Croydon" — and it's right on the next call.

03

Forward only the calls you choose

Start with after‑hours and overflow. Move it onto the main line once you've listened to a week of real calls. Turn it off in one click.

FAQ

Before you put it on your line.

Will my customers know it's not a person?
Most don't notice on a short booking call. If they ask, the agent says straight away that it's an assistant and offers to put them through. We don't let it claim to be human.
What happens if it doesn't understand someone?
It asks once more. If it's still unclear it stops trying and transfers, or takes a number and flags the call for a human callback. It won't loop a caller round in circles.
Do I have to change my phone number?
No. You keep your number and forward the calls you want handled — all of them, or just the ones that ring out after four rings. It's a carrier setting, reversible any time.
Can it handle accents and noisy job sites?
It's the thing we test hardest. Before you go live you'll hear recordings from your own trial calls, so you're judging it on your customers rather than on a demo.
What if it books something I can't do?
It can only offer slots your calendar says are free, inside your service area, for services on your list. Anything outside those rules goes to a human instead of being booked.
How long until it's working properly?
Setup is under an hour once we have your price list. Most businesses run it on after‑hours calls for a week, listen back, tune the wording, then move it onto the main line.
