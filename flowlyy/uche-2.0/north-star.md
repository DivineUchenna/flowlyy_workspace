# North Star

Two things everything else serves. If a task doesn't move one of these, it's
probably admin, content, or noise — treat it accordingly.

## 1. Zero operatives turned away at the gate

The certification-compliance-tracker exists so that no worker on a client's
site is ever refused entry because a CSCS, CISRS, CPCS, IPAF, PASMA, SMSTS,
SSSTS, Gas Safe, NICEIC (or other) certification lapsed without anyone
noticing. Every renewal gets flagged before it expires, every client always
knows who's compliant and who isn't. This is the product promise — the reason
the certification tracker is the lead product, not just one of seven.

## 2. First paying client, live

Get Flowlyy's first real client onboarded, paying, and running on the
tracker — proof the product works and the founder-rate pitch (£400/month,
first 5 clients) converts. This is directional, not a countdown: there's no
fixed deadline attached, it's the thing outreach, content, and product work
are all ultimately pointed at until it happens. Once it does, the north star
becomes proving the model repeats.

and niche choices that support this.
